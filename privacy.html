YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, made clear.

How Neighbour handles information when you visit our website or get in touch with us.

Updated 10 September 2026

Overview

Neighbour respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, company and the details you choose to share. We may also collect limited technical data, such as browser type and pages visited, to understand and improve the website.

How we use information

  • To respond to questions and project enquiries.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers where needed to operate our business, subject to appropriate safeguards. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information that we hold about you, subject to applicable law. Contact us at contact@neighbour.sg.
