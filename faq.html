QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions.

A few useful answers about how we work, what we build and what it is like to partner with Neighbour.

HOW WE WORK
What does Neighbour build?

We design and engineer digital products, including websites, web applications, mobile experiences and internal business tools.

Can you take a product from idea to launch?

Yes. We can help shape the strategy, validate the experience, design the interface, build the product and support it after launch.

Do you work with existing teams?

Absolutely. We often work alongside product, design and engineering teams, filling a specific capability gap or accelerating a key initiative.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused first release often takes 8–16 weeks. The right schedule depends on scope, technical complexity and how much discovery is needed.

Where is Neighbour based?

We are based in Singapore and collaborate with teams across the region and beyond.

How do we get started?

Send us a short note about your idea or challenge. We will arrange an introductory conversation and recommend a practical next step.
